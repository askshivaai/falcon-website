The real problem is not accounting. It is five different versions of your numbers.

Invoices are in one tool. Stock is in another register. Bank statements come later. Salaries sit in a sheet. GST is worked out at month-end. Your accounts team spends its time making all of these agree.

08 · TrustWhy your accountant can trust it

The checks are built in, not hidden in settings.

Roles, approvals, checks before anything is final, health checks on the books, a history of every change, proper corrections, month closing and clear limits on the AI. All of it is part of Falcon from day one.

  • 01

    Roles decide who can do what

    What each person can prepare, approve or record depends on their role.

  • 02

    Approvals

    Important entries can go to a second person for approval before they become final.

  • 03

    Checked before final

    An entry starts as a draft. It is checked, approved, and only then recorded in the books.

  • 04

    Health checks on the books

    Falcon keeps checking that the books add up and shows problems early, not months later.

  • 05

    History of every change

    Who did what, and when, is always kept.

  • 06

    Corrections done properly

    A wrong entry is corrected with a new entry and a note. Nothing is quietly edited.

  • 07

    Month closing

    Closing or reopening a month is a deliberate step that needs permission.

  • 08

    AI limits

    The AI can only do a fixed list of things. Every action is checked, previewed and confirmed by a person.

These are things Falcon does. We do not claim certificates we do not have. What is covered for GST and tax is confirmed with you before you start.
