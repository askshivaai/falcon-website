We build money software that a business owner can actually understand.

01What we believe

Five ideas we never compromise on.

  1. 01

    One set of true numbers.

    Sales, purchases, bank, stock, salaries, tax and the books all come from the same place.

  2. 02

    Check before it is final.

    Every entry is prepared, checked and approved before it goes into the books.

  3. 03

    AI helps with the books. It does not keep them.

    The AI explains and drafts. Falcon's fixed rules do the recording and the totals.

  4. 04

    The history is part of the job.

    Who did what, when, and what changed is always kept.

  5. 05

    New parts connect to the same books.

    Adding stock or payroll never creates a separate system you have to match later.
