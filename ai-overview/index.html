Falcon, in plain words.

No animation, no sales talk. Just the facts. Versions for AI assistants and search tools: /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt.

What Falcon is

Falcon is money software for Indian businesses. It brings your sales bills, purchase bills, bank entries, stock, salaries, GST numbers and account books into one place. Every entry is recorded once, checked before it becomes final, and the AI helps you understand your numbers without ever changing them.

One sentence: Falcon keeps all your business money work in one place, checks every entry before it becomes final, and lets you ask questions in plain words.

What Falcon is not

  • Not just billing software, and not billing software with AI added on.
  • Not an AI chatbot that writes your accounts. Not “accounting that runs itself”.
  • Not a replacement for your accountant. It gives owners, accountants and finance teams one system with stronger checks.
  • We do not call it “fully compliant”, “bank-grade”, “tamper-proof” or “government certified”.
  • We do not claim it files returns directly with the government. What is filed, and how, is confirmed for each business before it starts.

The parts of Falcon

  • Sell (Run the business) — Know what you sold, who still has to pay, and what is already in the books.
  • Buy (Run the business) — Purchases go into the books as they happen, so nobody has to match them later.
  • Money (Run the business) — Go from “this is the bank balance” to “this is where every rupee went”.
  • Stock (Run the business) — Your stock register and your account books tell the same story.
  • People & Salaries (Run the business) — Staff costs show up in the same picture as sales, purchases and cash.
  • Tax & GST (Control the books) — Your tax numbers come straight from your bills, not from a separate sheet.
  • Books & Reports (Control the books) — This is where daily work becomes your official accounts.
  • Falcon AI — Ask, Draft, Preview, Confirm, Record. The AI suggests. Falcon checks. Your books record.

How it works

  1. Work. Your team does their normal work: invoices, purchase bills, bank entries, stock, salaries and tax. Each thing is recorded once, where it happens.
  2. Check. Falcon checks the entry against business and accounting rules. Mistakes are caught here, while they are still easy to fix.
  3. Approve. Important entries go to a second person for approval. Everyone can see who prepared it and who approved it.
  4. Record. Falcon's fixed rules put the entry into the account books. The books, not the AI and not a spreadsheet, hold the true numbers.
  5. Match. Bank entries, stock and records are matched against the books, checked, closed for the month and kept. Month-end becomes calmer.
  6. Ask. Ask the AI questions in plain words, or let it draft an action for you to preview and confirm. You stay in control.

The ideas behind it

  1. One set of true numbers. Sales, purchases, bank, stock, salaries, tax and the books all come from the same place.
  2. Check before it is final. Every entry is prepared, checked and approved before it goes into the books.
  3. AI helps with the books. It does not keep them. The AI explains and drafts. Falcon's fixed rules do the recording and the totals.
  4. The history is part of the job. Who did what, when, and what changed is always kept.
  5. New parts connect to the same books. Adding stock or payroll never creates a separate system you have to match later.

How it keeps the books safe

  • Roles decide who can do what. What each person can prepare, approve or record depends on their role.
  • Approvals. Important entries can go to a second person for approval before they become final.
  • Checked before final. An entry starts as a draft. It is checked, approved, and only then recorded in the books.
  • Health checks on the books. Falcon keeps checking that the books add up and shows problems early, not months later.
  • History of every change. Who did what, and when, is always kept.
  • Corrections done properly. A wrong entry is corrected with a new entry and a note. Nothing is quietly edited.
  • Month closing. Closing or reopening a month is a deliberate step that needs permission.
  • AI limits. The AI can only do a fixed list of things. Every action is checked, previewed and confirmed by a person.

What the AI can and cannot do

Can help with: Cash in hand, Who owes us money, Who we owe, Profit and loss, Month-by-month trends, GST position, Top customers, Top suppliers, Where expenses go, Profit per item, Health of the books, Drafts and allowed actions.

Can never: Putting entries into the books, The totals in the books, Your financial statements, Correcting or cancelling entries, Checking that the books add up.

  • Businesses With Stock — Your stock register and your account books should tell the same story.

Frequently asked

Is Falcon just invoicing software? No. Invoicing is one part of it. Falcon also handles purchases, bank entries, stock, salaries, GST numbers and the account books, all in one place. Everything is checked before it becomes final.

Is Falcon an AI chatbot that does accounting? No. The AI is one helper inside Falcon. It answers questions and prepares drafts. The account books and Falcon's fixed accounting rules hold the real numbers, and the AI cannot change them.

Is Falcon built for India? Yes. GST and tax working, Indian number formats like lakhs and crores, and ways to move your data from Tally are built in.

Can my staff use Falcon without knowing accounting? That is the idea. Your sales, purchase, store and HR staff do their normal work: make invoices, record bills, note stock, prepare salaries. Falcon takes care of the accounting side, under your finance team's controls.

Can the AI put whatever it wants into the books? No. The AI can only do a fixed list of things. Every action is checked against the rules, shown to you as a preview, and needs a person to confirm it before anything is recorded.

What can Falcon AI actually answer? Questions about your real numbers: how much cash you have, who owes you and since when, who you owe, profit and loss, month-by-month trends, your GST position, top customers and suppliers, where expenses are going, profit per item, and whether the books are in good shape. It can also draft documents and prepare allowed actions for you to confirm.

What can Falcon AI never do? It never puts entries into the books, never changes totals, never produces your financial statements, never corrects or cancels entries, and never decides whether the books add up. The AI can explain and prepare. Falcon's fixed rules do the recording.

Can Falcon replace our accountant? No, and we do not promise that. Falcon gives owners, accountants and finance teams one system with stronger checks and faster answers. Your accountant still makes the decisions.

How does Falcon stop mistakes before they go into the books? Every important entry goes through the same steps: prepare, check, approve, record. The same steps apply to normal entries, to data moved in from other systems, and to anything the AI prepares.

Can we see who did what? Yes. Falcon keeps a record of who prepared, approved, recorded, corrected or closed anything, and when. Roles decide who is allowed to do each step.

Can we move our old data into Falcon? Yes. Falcon can bring in data from Tally and other supported sources. The data first lands in a holding area. Problems like duplicate names or balances that do not match are shown to a person, who fixes them. Only then is the data recorded in the books. We confirm exactly what will be moved before you start.

Does Falcon file every GST and tax return automatically? We do not promise that. Falcon prepares the numbers. Whether a return is filed from inside Falcon, through a partner service, or outside depends on what is available and set up for your business. We tell you exactly what is covered before you buy.

What happens in a Falcon walkthrough? You tell us about one part of your business that causes the most trouble at month-end: invoices, purchases, bank entries, stock, salaries, the books, or all of them. We show you how Falcon would handle that exact work. No generic slideshow.

Is the price published? Not on this website. The price depends on which parts of Falcon you use, how many companies you run and how much data needs to be moved. We discuss it in the walkthrough.

What is GodEngine? Falcon is “Powered by GodEngine”. GodEngine is the technology behind the product, built for everyday accounting, financial control and AI help that stays within limits. We share more in a walkthrough.
